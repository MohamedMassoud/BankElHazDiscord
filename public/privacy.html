What we collect

Bank El Haz is a Discord Activity. Using the Discord Embedded App SDK, it reads your Discord user ID, display name (global name or username) and avatar to identify you in the game and show your profile.

Game data

Player names, balances, moves, emotes and other game state are sent to the game relay and stored in a Cloudflare Durable Object for the duration of the room. This data is used only to synchronize the game between players and may persist while the room is active so players can reconnect. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your Discord password, email address, or private messages.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Local storage

Your browser stores preferences (language, theme, audio settings) locally on your device. Avatar images are proxied through the game host.

Children

This game is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age required by Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this URL.
